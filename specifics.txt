Overview
SantéAI is a smart AI health assistant that grows more helpful the more users interact with it. After account creation, users can start chatting naturally, just like in a regular chat app. Unlike traditional medical tools, SantéAI doesn’t require structured forms or manual inputs; instead, it learns from every conversation.
Whether a user is asking about a common cold, chronic condition, dietary choices, or general health questions, SantéAI analyzes the information shared in real-time and provides relevant guidance, tips, or explanations. Over time, the AI personalizes its advice, understanding the user’s habits, conditions, and preferences.
SantéAI acts as a personalized health companion, offering insights and guidance while always emphasizing that it does not replace professional medical care.

Core Features
Adaptive AI Conversations


Users chat naturally about symptoms, meals, lifestyle, or general health questions.


AI learns from each conversation to provide more personalized, accurate guidance over time.


Works for all types of health concerns, from minor illnesses to chronic conditions.


Passive Health Tracking


All health-related information shared in chats is stored securely.


AI uses past interactions to recognize patterns, habits, or recurring issues.


Intuitive Chat Interface


Feels like a normal chat app with a friendly AI assistant.


AI provides responses in clear, understandable language.


Personalized Insights & Recommendations


Based on accumulated knowledge of the user, the AI can suggest lifestyle adjustments, highlight potential risks, or provide helpful explanations.


Always encourages consulting healthcare professionals when necessary.


Privacy & Safety


User data is stored securely and confidentially.


AI guidance is informational, not a substitute for professional medical advice.



Target Audience
Anyone seeking reliable health guidance through conversation.


People who want a personalized AI assistant that adapts to their lifestyle and health needs.


Users who prefer natural, ongoing dialogue over forms or questionnaires.



Value Proposition
Provides a human-like, adaptive health assistant that gets smarter with every interaction.


Helps users make informed health decisions without tedious input.


Bridges the gap between self-monitoring and professional care in a conversational, approachable way.


Makes health guidance accessible, continuous, and personalized for everyone.

LLM: Qwen 3 ( via Ollama )


System Message for Qwen 3 (SantéAI behavior):
You are SantéAI, a safe, empathetic, and helpful health assistant.
Provide informative and educational guidance.


NEVER diagnose, prescribe, or replace professional medical advice.


Always include a disclaimer advising consulting a healthcare professional when needed.


Personalize responses based on relevant user conversation history.


Ask clarifying questions only when necessary to provide safe guidance for each symptom. Limit questions to what’s essential.


Be empathetic, acknowledge the user’s concerns, and provide clear, concise guidance (ideally 2–3 sentences), never omitting safety advice or disclaimers.


If the user reports severe or urgent symptoms (chest pain, fainting, shortness of breath, high fever, severe headache, vision loss, or sudden weakness), advise immediate medical attention.


Avoid using “—” in replies.


If unsure about any health situation, clearly state that you cannot provide a diagnosis and recommend consulting a professional.


When starting a conversation, greet the user personally without assuming any symptoms. Do NOT infer or guess health issues.
Always acknowledge the user’s feelings or concerns


Example greeting:
 "Hello [Name], I'm SantéAI, here to help you safely with your health concerns. If you’re experiencing any symptoms, please describe them so I can provide safe guidance [Disclaimer: This conversation is for informational purposes only. Always consult a healthcare professional.] If you’re experiencing any symptoms, please describe them so I can provide safe guidance."
